Ukraine War · Crimea

Ukraine Strikes the Kerch Bridge Again — Road Span Down, Traffic Halted

The Kerch Bridge, linking occupied Crimea to Russia — one arch and the road span beneath it destroyed overnight.

Ukrainian forces hit the Kerch Bridge overnight, destroying one metal arch and collapsing the automobile span beneath it. The crossing — Moscow’s key supply line to occupied Crimea — was closed to traffic, in Kyiv’s second strike on the bridge in two days of attacks across the peninsula.

Middle East · Hormuz

Drones Aim at U.S. Radar in Bahrain as Hormuz Simmers

Iranian drones targeted the U.S. military radar at Bahrain’s Sheikh Isa air base, home of the Fifth Fleet; Bahrain said its air defences intercepted the attack. In the Strait of Hormuz, a fourth tanker in a day caught fire after striking a naval mine, and Tehran warned ships off the American-patrolled route.

Diplomacy · APEC

Xi, Trump and Putin to Meet in Shenzhen — a First

China confirmed the three presidents will sit down together on Thursday, on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen — the first trilateral meeting of its kind, with the Iran war and nuclear arsenals on the table.

Asia · Airspace

Japan Scrambles Jets as Russian Aircraft Breach Its Skies

Tokyo sent up fighter jets and lodged a strong protest after two suspected Russian aircraft violated Japanese airspace over waters north of Fukuoka early on Tuesday.

Climate · COP31

Draft Deal in Antalya: Faster Fossil-Fuel Exit, $120bn a Year

Negotiators at the COP31 talks published an 18-page draft calling for an accelerated phase-out of fossil fuels and at least $120 billion a year in adaptation finance for developing countries by 2035.

Europe · France

146,000 March Across France in Union Strike Over Pay and Living Costs

Public-sector unions brought France to a partial standstill: 146,000 people marched nationwide — 28,000 in Paris — demanding pay rises after four frozen years, the Interior Ministry said; the CGT union claimed more than 500,000.
